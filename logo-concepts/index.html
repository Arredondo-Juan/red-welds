Red Welds logo concepts

Drafts based on the sketch: the R's leg becomes the first stroke of the W, so the two letters share one line. Each one is shown on black, white and red to check it works everywhere.

A · One continuous line

The R's leg runs straight into the W as one unbroken zigzag, like a single weld pass. Sharp corners to match the current logo. The R keeps its red stem and curve; its leg is already part of the white W.

B · Closest to the sketch

Separate strokes with rounded ends, like weld beads or rods. The R's leg sits parallel to the W's first stroke instead of joining it, the way the sketch draws it.

C · Concept A with the name

The full logo for the sign, truck and shirts, with "Red Welds" set under the mark like the current one.
